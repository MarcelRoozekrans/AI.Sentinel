# Security policy

AI.Sentinel follows the [security policy of the product line](https://github.com/MarcelRoozekrans/product-line/blob/main/SECURITY.md). This page is the short version for this repository.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report privately through GitHub: open the [Security tab](https://github.com/MarcelRoozekrans/AI.Sentinel/security) of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Include what you found, the affected package and version, and the steps to reproduce it.

## What happens next

* You get a confirmation of receipt within 5 working days.
* You get a first assessment, including severity, within 15 working days.
* The aim is to fix the issue and disclose it together with you within 90 days, sooner for critical findings.
* You are credited in the advisory if you want to be.

## Supported versions

* The latest release receives security fixes.
* After a new major version, the previous major still receives fixes for critical and high findings for 6 months. After that, only the latest release.

## Scope

Good-faith research that follows this policy is welcome. Third-party services, volumetric denial of service, and bypassing a guardrail on your own machine, where you already control the process, are out of scope; the [shared policy](https://github.com/MarcelRoozekrans/product-line/blob/main/SECURITY.md#out-of-scope) has the details.
